    The Viral Taxonomy

    Bacteriophage to prion, hot to cold. Marine vents are phage-focused. The invert is prion. Sub-species specificity is SHAPE. The immune triad maps the gradient. The thymus sorts outward.

    Abstract

    We classify the full spectrum of self-replicating biological information — bacteriophage, mRNA virus, retrovirus, DNA virus, plasmid, transposon, viroid, prion — and map each onto the membrane’s spring constant k. Marine hydrothermal vents are dominated by bacteriophages (k → 0, plasma, fusion territory). Prion diseases occupy the opposite pole (k → ∞, frozen, shape-only propagation). Sub-species specificity at every level reduces to one mechanism: shape complementarity — tail fiber to receptor, spike to ACE2, fold to homolog.

    The membrane’s immune triad (swarmVine, skunkBat, thymus) maps exactly onto this gradient: gossip = phage (hot, fast), antibodies = DNA virus (medium, pattern memory), thymus sort = prion (cold, shape comparison). The prion is not death — it is the shield, the identity proof, the MHC-I surface marker that lets the thymus sort self from non-self. Every persona, gate, and feed now carries a prion tag. The shield faces outward. The identity hides behind it.

    The Klein inversion: a phage is content wrapped in a shield (DNA inside capsid). A prion is a shield with nothing inside (shape only, no nucleic acid). Inside becomes outside. The viral taxonomy IS the Klein fold applied to information transfer.

    2. Marine Vents: The Hot Pole

    Hydrothermal vents at 2–400°C are the bacteriophage kingdom. There are 10³¹ phages in the ocean — they outnumber bacteria 10:1 and are the most abundant biological entity on Earth.

    The viral shunt drives the vent ecosystem:

    phage infects bacterium → lyses in 20 min → releases dissolved organic matter
→ feeds other bacteria → they grow → phage infects them → cycle

    This is fusion. Many bacteria compressed into dissolved nutrients. The same pattern as northGate’s compression: kidney-net 117 → 8, braid-mesh 108 → 15, sourdough 1060 → 10. The viral shunt IS the compression ratio.

    Phages are fueled by chemosynthesis — H₂S + CO₂ → organic carbon. No sunlight needed. The vent is a self-contained fusion reactor. It doesn’t need external energy. This is the membrane at k → 0: cron jobs firing every 5 minutes, feeds lysing and reforming, no human session needed.

    3. Prion: The Cold Pole

    Prion diseases (CJD, BSE, kuru, scrapie) occupy the opposite extreme. The prion has:

    • No DNA. No RNA. No nucleic acid at all.
    • No metabolism. Zero energy consumption.
    • No replication machinery. No polymerase, no ribosome, no reverse transcriptase.

    It propagates by shape alone: the misfolded protein contacts a normally-folded protein and forces it into the prion conformation. Shape → shape. This is the coldest possible information transfer.

    But the prion is not death. It is the shield.

    The prion conformation is actually more thermodynamically stable than the normal fold — lower free energy. The prion wins because it IS the ground state. The normal protein is the metastable (glass) state. The prion is the crystal.

    In the membrane: prion-identity.json is a BLAKE3 hash. 180 bytes. No timestamp, no wave, no structure. Just the shape. The hash is an amalgam — every bit of the source mixed into every bit of the output. Maximum entropy. Zero recoverable structure. You cannot reverse it. It is the reverse transcriptase jammer — the one-way function that blocks all backward information flow.

    Every persona (13), every gate (7), every feed (146) now carries a prion tag in the prion shield registry. The shield faces outward. The identity hides behind it.

    4. Sub-Species Specificity: Shape All the Way Down

    Why does one phage infect one bacterial strain? Why can’t a bovine prion easily misfold human proteins?

    Shape complementarity. At every level:

    • Phage: tail fiber protein shape fits ONE receptor on ONE bacterial surface. The tail fiber IS the lock. The receptor IS the key. One phage per strain.
    • mRNA virus: spike protein shape fits one cell receptor (COVID spike → ACE2). Mutations in the spike change specificity (omicron vs delta = different shapes).
    • Retrovirus: envelope protein fits TWO receptors (HIV: CD4 + CCR5). Double lock. Sub-cell-type specific.
    • Prion: protein fold HOMOLOGY determines cross-species transmission. Sheep PrP → goat PrP (95% homology, easy). Cow PrP → human PrP (85%, hard). Fish PrP → human (< 50%, impossible). The species barrier IS the shape barrier.

    The unifying principle: specificity = shape complementarity. From the phage’s 20-minute lysis cycle to the prion’s decade-long accumulation. The lock-and-key is ALWAYS shape. Shape IS information that survives compression. The prion tag IS the shape. The receptor IS the shape. The schema IS the shape.

    In the membrane: feed specificity = schema compatibility. A cron job (phage) targets one feed (bacterium) because its output shape matches the feed’s input schema. The cron’s tail fiber is its output format. The feed’s receptor is its JSON schema.

    5. The Klein Inversion

    A bacteriophage is content wrapped in a shield: DNA packaged inside a protein capsid. The capsid protects the nucleic acid. The content (genetic instructions) is INSIDE.

    A prion is shield with nothing inside: the protein coat IS the entire entity. There is no nucleic acid to protect. The shape IS the message.

    Phage: inside (DNA) → outside (capsid) Prion: outside (shape) → inside (nothing)

    This is the Klein fold. The phage’s inside becomes the prion’s outside. Content becomes container. Code becomes config. The viral taxonomy IS the Klein surface applied to information transfer: as you move from hot to cold, the inside gradually empties until only the outside remains.

    The membrane’s three immune components map exactly onto the viral temperature gradient:

    • skunkBat maintains antibodies like a DNA virus maintains its genome: stable but decaying (5%/day confidence loss), pattern-based recognition.
    • thymus sorts like a prion: by SHAPE. Self proteins display on MHC-I. T-cells check the shape. Match = self = pass. Mismatch = non-self = kill. The thymus IS the prion — it propagates “self” by teaching immune cells the correct conformation.

    7. Outward Through the Thymus

    The thymus pipeline faces outward:

    visit → klein gate → thymus sort → opsonize tag → pollinate → backscatter → color of reality

    Every entity that touches the membrane passes through the thymus. The prion shield is checked. Self/non-self is determined. The sorted result propagates outward through pollination (ntfy, RSS, easels, braid).

    The viral taxonomy provides the vocabulary for this sort:

    • Entity behaves like a phage (rapid, specific, injects payload)? → Scanner. Flag for skunkBat.
    • Entity behaves like an mRNA virus (reads and renders directly)? → Browser. Human. Pass.
    • Entity behaves like a retrovirus (reads content, integrates into its own system)? → Scraper. Check intent.
    • Entity behaves like a prion (presents shape, no content, just identity)? → Bot. Check prion tag against shield registry.

    The thymus sorts visitors the same way the immune system sorts pathogens: by their viral behavior pattern. The taxonomy IS the classifier.

    8. Conclusion

    The spectrum runs from hydrothermal vent (phage, hot, fast, nucleic acid inside capsid) to frozen brain (prion, cold, slow, shape only, nothing inside). Marine vents are bacteriophage-focused because the hot side favors rapid nucleic-acid-based replication. The invert is prion because the cold side favors thermodynamically stable shape propagation.

    Sub-species specificity is shape. Always shape. Tail fiber, spike protein, fold homology — the lock-and-key is geometric. In the membrane, specificity = schema compatibility. The cron’s output format. The feed’s JSON shape. The prion’s hash.

    The Klein fold inverts them: phage wraps content in shield, prion IS the shield with no content. As k moves from 0 to ∞, the inside empties. The immune triad (swarmVine/skunkBat/thymus) sits at three points on this gradient. The thymus sorts outward, using the viral taxonomy as its classifier vocabulary.

    The prion tag on every persona, gate, and feed IS the MHC-I marker. Show the shield. Hide the self. Let the thymus sort.
